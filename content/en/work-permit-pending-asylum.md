## First: file the asylum application properly

The clock does not start when you decide to seek asylum. It starts when your **Form I-589 is properly filed**. If your case is in court, that means filing it with the immigration judge, not USCIS, and having it entered in the record. Ask for the stamped receipt and keep it: it proves the date that counts.

If you filed affirmatively with USCIS and the case later moved to court, the original date usually carries over — but that is exactly the sort of detail worth confirming with a lawyer.

## The waiting period

The law bars an asylum applicant from receiving employment authorization immediately: a waiting period runs from filing. The exact length and the conditions attached **have changed several times in recent years**, through rulemaking and federal litigation.

So the practical rule: **before preparing the I-765, confirm the period currently in force** on the USCIS page for asylum-based employment authorization, or with a lawyer. Filing early means rejection, and weeks lost with it.

## The asylum clock, and how it stops

USCIS and EOIR keep an internal counter known as the **asylum clock**. It stops when delay is attributable to you:

- Requesting a continuance to find counsel or prepare.
- Declining the first individual hearing date offered.
- Failing to file documents or translations by the deadline.
- Missing a biometrics appointment.

System delays should not stop the count. In practice the clock is sometimes stopped in error, and you can ask the court to correct it — worth catching early.

There is a real tension here: requesting a continuance may be right for your asylum case and simultaneously delay your work permit. That is a decision to make with a lawyer, not in the courthouse hallway.

## The application: Form I-765

- **Category**: **(c)(8)**, pending asylum.
- **Documents**: a copy of the I-589 with its receipt stamp, photo identification, passport-style photos per the instructions, and proof of the filing date.
- **Fee**: I-765 fees have changed repeatedly. Verify the exact amount and fee waiver options on the USCIS fee page the day you file. An incorrect payment gets the entire package returned.
- **Filing**: to the address in the current instructions, or online where the category allows.

Once approved you receive an EAD card with an expiration date, which also lets you apply for a Social Security number.

## Renewals

The EAD for pending asylum renews while the case remains open. File the renewal **well before** expiration; processing times vary widely. Automatic extensions exist for certain categories when the renewal is filed on time — confirm whether yours qualifies.

If asylum is granted, your situation changes: asylees are authorized to work by virtue of status, though keeping a document that proves it to employers is sensible.

## If asylum is denied

While a BIA appeal is pending, the permit may remain renewable. Once the order becomes final, it ends. People granted [withholding or CAT protection](/en/defensive-asylum/) can obtain work authorization, through a different route.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I work while waiting?**
Not immediately. Wait the current period, then file the I-765 under C08.

**What stops the clock?**
Delays you cause, such as a continuance or declining the first date offered.

**What does it cost?**
Fees change; confirm the amount and waiver options with USCIS before filing.
