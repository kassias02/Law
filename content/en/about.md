## Who edits this site

Deportation Defense Law is edited by **Hassan Benmouloud**, an independent consultant, through Loom Digital LLC (Montana, United States). I am not an attorney, not a notario, and not a DOJ-accredited representative. Nothing published here is legal advice, and no one at this site can represent you in immigration court.

What I do: gather what official sources say about removal proceedings, in plain English and Spanish, and connect people with licensed immigration attorneys.

## Where the information comes from

Every guide is written from primary sources, cited with direct links at the foot of each page:

- The **Immigration and Nationality Act** and the **Code of Federal Regulations** (8 C.F.R.).
- **EOIR** materials, including the Immigration Court Practice Manual.
- Official forms and instructions from **USCIS**, **ICE** and **EOIR**.
- Published decisions of the **Supreme Court** and the **Board of Immigration Appeals**.
- Research from established organizations such as the American Immigration Council.

Pages carry a last-reviewed date. Immigration law moves fast, especially on detention and bond: if a page has not been reviewed in months, treat it as a starting point and confirm with an attorney.

## How the contact form works

When you submit the form, your information goes to **a licensed immigration law firm** covering your area and case type, so they can contact you. We charge nothing to the people who write in. Firms that receive cases pay a flat advertising fee; that fee never depends on whether you hire the attorney or on what you pay them.

We are not a comprehensive directory and this is not an endorsement. Compare firms, ask for fees in writing, and verify any attorney's license with the state bar before signing anything.
